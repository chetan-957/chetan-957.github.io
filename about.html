🙋 About me

Data, done right.

I'm Chetan Injavarapu, an Operations Data Analyst with a Master's in Data Science from Rutgers University (4.0 GPA). My work sits at the intersection of operations analytics, Power BI dashboard development, and data modeling — turning messy operational datasets into KPI-driven reporting that people actually use to make decisions.

Before grad school, I earned a B.Tech in Computer Science and interned as a Data Science Intern, building ML models and analytical solutions with Python and SQL. At Rutgers, I managed building operations for Housing & Residence Life — tracking service requests, improving response workflows, and keeping operational records clean. That operations experience is exactly what makes my analytics practical: I've been the person relying on the data, not just building the dashboard.

My projects range from a Power BI solution analyzing $45M+ in retail sales to geospatial analyses spanning 180+ countries and 58 California counties. I care about data validation, reporting standards, and governance — the unglamorous work that makes analysis trustworthy.
